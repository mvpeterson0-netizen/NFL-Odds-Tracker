Stix: NFL player prop research

Stix puts real sportsbook player props next to what actually happened, for every starting QB, RB, WR and TE, every game of the 2026 season, so you can do your own research before you bet.

Open Stix

What you can do

  • Board: this week's lines for passing, rushing and receiving props, with each player's recent results.
  • Player pages: every game's pre-game line next to the actual stat, line movement through the week, usage, matchup and weather.
  • Research: build filters and ready-made leads, such as big line moves or usage changes.
  • Results: how often overs and unders hit, by stat and position.
  • Defense: how each defense ranks against each position.
  • My picks (free account): save picks, locked at kickoff, and track your record in units.
  • Alerts: get a notification when the week's lines are posted.

Where the data comes from

Lines are DraftKings and FanDuel player props from The Odds API, captured about 3 hours before kickoff (not closing lines). Stats, injuries, snaps, depth charts and rosters come from nflverse, and weather from Open-Meteo. Stix uses real data only; anything unavailable is labeled rather than filled in.

Good to know

  • Stix is a research tool, not betting advice. Flagged props are leads, not predictions.
